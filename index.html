Why OSEL?

👤 Attribution

Mandatory credit. Anyone who redistributes your work must keep your name and contact information visible.

🚫 Anti-AI Training

Protects your creativity. Strictly prohibits data scraping and using your work to train AI or Large Language Models (LLMs).

🛡️ Ethical Use

Peace of mind. Zero permission for use in military weapons, warfare tech, computer viruses, or malware.

💰 Fair Share

100% free for indie creators and small businesses making under $500,000/year. Large corporations must pay a royalty fee.
